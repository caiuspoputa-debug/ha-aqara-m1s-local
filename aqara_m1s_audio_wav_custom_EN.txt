Aqara M1S - Custom WAV audio, arm_ok.wav and local playback
================================================================

Purpose:
- convert MP3/WAV into a format accepted by Aqara M1S
- test sound directly on the hub
- replace the internal arm_ok.wav sound
- avoid the issue where aplay plays the sound 3 times
- control volume correctly through the WAV file, not through HomeKit


============================================================
1. Accepted WAV format
============================================================

Recommended format for Aqara M1S:

mono
32000 Hz
PCM S32_LE

Example final file:

arm_ok.wav


============================================================
2. Conversion with ffmpeg
============================================================

Convert from an MP3/WAV source into Aqara M1S format:

ffmpeg -y -i input.mp3 -ac 1 -ar 32000 -filter:a "volume=0.70" -c:a pcm_s32le arm_ok.wav

Explanation:
- -ac 1 = mono
- -ar 32000 = 32 kHz
- volume=0.70 = volume reduced to 70%
- pcm_s32le = PCM 32-bit little-endian, accepted by M1S

For quieter audio:

volume=0.56

For louder audio:

volume=0.80


============================================================
3. HA location for hub download
============================================================

Place the file in Home Assistant:

/config/www/arm_ok.wav

The URL becomes:

http://HOME_ASSISTANT_IP:8123/local/arm_ok.wav

Example:

http://192.168.0.20:8123/local/arm_ok.wav


============================================================
4. Download to hub for testing
============================================================

In Telnet on the hub:

cd /tmp
rm -f arm_ok_new.wav
wget http://192.168.0.20:8123/local/arm_ok.wav -O arm_ok_new.wav
ls -l arm_ok_new.wav


============================================================
5. Test playback only once
============================================================

IMPORTANT:
On this firmware, the simple command:

aplay arm_ok_new.wav

may play the sound 3 times.

The correct command for one playback is:

aplay -x 1 /tmp/arm_ok_new.wav


============================================================
6. Backup original sound
============================================================

The internal arm_ok.wav sound is here:

/data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav

Make a backup once:

cd /data/musics/music-scene
[ -f arm_ok.wav.bak ] || cp arm_ok.wav arm_ok.wav.bak
sync


============================================================
7. Replace arm_ok.wav permanently
============================================================

After the /tmp test sounds correct:

cp /tmp/arm_ok_new.wav /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav
chmod 775 /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav
sync

Final test:

aplay -x 1 /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav


============================================================
8. Restore original arm_ok.wav
============================================================

To restore the original sound:

cd /data/musics/music-scene
cp arm_ok.wav.bak arm_ok.wav
chmod 775 arm_ok.wav
sync
aplay -x 1 arm_ok.wav


============================================================
9. Internal sound list
============================================================

Find internal WAV files:

find /data/musics -type f -name "*.wav" 2>/dev/null

Important locations:

/data/musics/music-scene/
/data/musics/music-us/
/data/musics/music-ch/

Useful examples:

/data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav
/data/musics/music-scene/arm_start.wav
/data/musics/music-scene/disarm.wav
/data/musics/music-scene/door_bell_1.wav
/data/musics/music-scene/alarm.wav


============================================================
10. Volume
============================================================

Important discovery:

HomeKit / Aqara volume:

number.aqarahublight_volume
number.aqara_hub_m1s_2653_volume

and the property:

persist.sys.volume

do NOT control the volume for direct playback with:

aplay -x 1

Therefore, for directly played local sounds, the volume must be reduced inside the WAV file.

Correct solution:
- make the WAV quieter during conversion
- use volume=0.70, 0.56, etc.
- do not rely on number.volume for aplay


============================================================
11. Quick commands
============================================================

Download:

wget http://192.168.0.20:8123/local/arm_ok.wav -O /tmp/arm_ok_new.wav

Test once:

aplay -x 1 /tmp/arm_ok_new.wav

Backup:

[ -f /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav.bak ] || cp /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav.bak

Replace:

cp /tmp/arm_ok_new.wav /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav
chmod 775 /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav
sync

Final test:

aplay -x 1 /data/musics/music-scene/arm_ok.wav
